CALCULATION RULES TO VALIDATE — Travel & Expense
Date: 27/10/2026

Seventeen calculation rules need your decision before we build them into Travel & Expense.
Each question gives the barème rule, a worked example and the options.
Write the chosen option on its Decision line.
Amounts follow barème V.2 2026, which HR confirmed is in force.


==========================================================================================
INTERNATIONAL PER DIEM
==========================================================================================

Q1. Per diem for stays of 15 days or more
    Under 15 days, the barème pays 140 € per day. From 15 days, it pays 1,200 € per month,
    prorated from the first day of the mission.
    Example: 14 days = 14 × 140 € = 1,960 €.  15 days = 1,200 € × 15 / 30 = 600 €.
      A. Apply the rule exactly as written: a 15-day stay pays less than a 14-day stay.
      B. Another rule (please describe it).
    Decision: ________

Q2. Number of days in a month for the proration
    The barème says the 1,200 € is prorated to the mission days, but not how many days a month counts.
    Example for 15 days: 30-day month = 600 €. 31-day month = 580.65 €. 28-day month = 642.86 €.
      A. Always 30 days.
      B. The real number of days of the month in which the trip starts.
    Decision: ________

Q3. Lodging on stays of 15 days or more
    The barème lists lodging only for stays under 15 days: UM6P hotel up to 300 € per night,
    hotel invoice up to 200 €, or an 85 € flat rate.
    Example for 15 days with the 85 € flat rate: 600 € without lodging,
    or 600 € + 14 nights × 85 € = 1,790 € with it.
      A. The 1,200 € monthly rate covers everything: no lodging is paid.
      B. Lodging is paid on top, as for shorter stays.
      C. UM6P books and pays the accommodation; no flat rate and no invoice.
    Decision: ________

Q4. Extensions and orders that follow each other
    Because 15 days pays less than 14, one long trip can be split into several orders to be paid more.
    Example: 28 days in one order = 1,120 €; as two 14-day orders = 3,920 €.
    A 14-day order extended by 1 day = 600 €; a separate 1-day order instead = 2,100 €.
      A. Count the per diem on the whole stay: extensions and orders that follow each other
         for the same person and destination are added together.
      B. Block a new order that overlaps or directly follows another one for the same destination.
      C. Count each order separately, which allows the split.
    Decision: ________

Q5. Doctoral students
    Barème V.2 2026 excludes doctoral students: their trips follow note de service 01262024.
    The platform currently applies the same barème to everyone.
      A. Doctoral students use the platform with their own barème.
         Please share the note and say how we identify a doctoral student.
      B. Doctoral students do not use this platform.

    Example, national day trip: 500 MAD is paid for a night that did not happen.
      A. No night, no lodging pay: nights = mission days − 1.

Q7. Hotel invoice above the cap
    The barème caps hotel invoices at 1,500 MAD per night in Morocco and 200 € per night abroad.
    The platform currently reimburses the full invoice.
    Example, 1 night in Morocco with a 5,000 MAD invoice: 1,500 MAD with the cap, 5,000 MAD today.
      A. Reimburse up to the cap per night; the collaborator pays the rest.
      B. Reimburse in full once the validators approve it.

Q8. Flat rate and hotel invoice together
    A collaborator who chose the lodging flat rate can still add a hotel invoice to the
    expense report. Both are currently paid.
    Example, 1 night in Morocco: 500 MAD flat rate + 1,500 MAD invoice = 2,000 MAD.
      A. One or the other: no hotel invoice when the flat rate was chosen or UM6P booked the hotel.

TRANSPORT
==========================================================================================

Q9. Which trips count for the mileage allowance
    The personal-car allowance is 3 MAD per km. The platform currently counts every trip
    entered, even repeated or unrelated to the mission.
    Example, mission in Casablanca: Benguerir → Casablanca and back = 344 km = 1,032 MAD.
    The same trips entered twice = 2,064 MAD. Trips to Oujda instead = 4,848 MAD.
      A. The trips must form one chain: assignment city → mission cities → back, each trip once.
      B. Trips stay free; the validators check them.
    Decision: ________

Q10. Airport transfer abroad
    The barème pays 50 € "per destination and per trip". The platform currently pays 50 € for
    each flight entered.
    Example: Casablanca → Paris → Casablanca = 2 flights = 100 €.
    The same flights entered 4 times = 400 €.
      A. 50 € at each arrival and each departure abroad, on the one-chain itinerary of Q9:
         Paris = 100 €.
      B. 50 € once per destination city: Paris = 50 €.
    Decision: ________

Q11. City transport allowance in Morocco
    The barème pays 100 MAD per day "only for people travelling by public transport".
    The platform currently pays it as soon as one train or bus trip is entered.
    Example: a 3-day mission by personal car, plus one short bus trip = 300 MAD.
      A. Only when the whole trip is made by public transport, with no personal or service car.
      B. As soon as one public transport trip is entered.
    Decision: ________

Q12. Mission hours used for meals
    In Morocco, a 120 MAD meal is paid when the mission covers 12:00–14:00 for lunch or
    19:30–21:30 for dinner. The hours come from the start and end times the collaborator declares.
    Example, one day: 08:00 to 18:00 = 1 lunch = 120 MAD.
    00:00 to 23:59 = lunch and dinner = 240 MAD.
      A. Keep the declared hours; the validators check them against the trips.
      B. Add departure and return times to the trips, and compute the meals from them.
